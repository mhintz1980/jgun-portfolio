## 4c. Hover, press and cursor effects (Codex source pass)

- [seen] Pointer followers: hero world pill and research video card use **per-render** `lerp(current,target,0.15)` for x/y, then a centred CSS translate. This is frame-based smoothing, with no duration or timed ease. Both debounce hover for 60 ms; hero left/right 32% zones select prev/next labels, centre selects world label. Their closing windows are 300 ms; research switches keep outgoing video for 600 ms. J[5808477,5811899), J[5896392,5899908). CSS reveal/wipe timings are separate rows below.
- [seen] Desktop model drag captures the pointer, accumulates pixel deltas, rotates by 0.12°/px (hero multiplier 0.5), clamps pitch −6°…36°, caps momentum ±55°/s, blends velocity by 0.65 per update and damps with `exp(-5.5*dt)` after release. Travel >6 px suppresses click. Coarse pointer disables this PL drag; mouse look is a dt-integrated spring (90 stiffness, `exp(-11*dt)` damping, ±12° influence), not OrbitControls. J[5716644,5716658), J[5788513,5794388), J[5813066,5814418), J[5822831,5823133). Research uses the same helper at full strength; hero drag is available only before outro. J[5900633,5901591), J[5907215,5907582).
- [seen] Splat hover raycasts a plane in the mesh's local frame, not individual splats. The point is footprint-clamped, velocity drives an attack/release envelope, and uniforms brighten, saturate and push splats sideways on XZ. Hover is disabled while dragging and after hero parking. J[5802659,5807152), J[5781740,5783410), J[5823715,5824871). Exact look-dependent envelope constants and shader inputs are in section 6.
- [seen] Home interactive CSS: Scroll to Start border/background 200 ms; hero arrow opacity 300 ms; research/product arrow fill 300 ms; bottom CTA backing fade 200 ms; press images scale 1 → 1.04 over 500 ms; footer text-link opacity 150 ms. Their HTML classes and actual stylesheet declarations are cited in section 5. Navbar link/plate/logo theme transitions are 200 ms linear. The menu button scales to 0.88 on press and hamburger bars rotate/translate on `aria-expanded`. H[9252,10200), H[17464,18100), H[46649,47000), H[61018,61500), H[67419,68000), H[87802,88200), H[94570,94950); C[64599,67384).

H[61018,61500), H[91563,92233). [seen] Cursor styles (`pointer`, `grab` etc.) are CSS states, not evidence of animated cursor geometry; the two DOM followers above are the authored cursor effects. ## 8. Accessibility and fallbacks  (any model)
- prefers-reduced-motion honored? **Yes, layered.** (1) `function hI(){return !matchMedia('(prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)').matches && !navigator.connection?.saveData}` gates heavy world building (also respects SaveData). (2) Lenis bundled with `respectReducedMotion` default true. (3) Transition classes check `prefersReducedMotion()`, skip the curtain animation and tear down. (4) CSS: `@media (prefers-reduced-motion:reduce){.marquee-track{animation:none}}`. (5) Per-component queries (collage class holds its own `reduced` MediaQueryList). A reduced-motion capture (1440x900, `reduce`) rendered the same hero content at rest. [seen]
- Text in DOM or only in canvas? **DOM.** `document.body.innerText` = 3,671 chars; real h1/h2/h3 headings; the hero headline is char-split *in the DOM* ("B u i l d Y o u r W o r l d s" — per-char spans driven by `data-text-animation`/`data-text-animation-state`), 132 `data-text-animation` elements. [seen]
- Pinch zoom allowed? **Yes.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width">` only — no `user-scalable=no`, no maximum-scale; only utility `touch-action:manipulation` in CSS, no `touch-action:none` locks found. [seen]
- No-WebGL fallback? **Graceful degradation, no dedicated fallback UI.** With `--disable-webgl --disable-webgl2 --disable-3d-apis`: page, nav, all text and the Lenis scroll container still work (3,659 chars innerText); canvases stay at default 300x150 (never sized); console logs `THREE.WebGLRenderer: A WebGL context could not be created` and `Footer world "Night Camp" failed to load`; no visible error/placeholder shown to the user. [seen]
