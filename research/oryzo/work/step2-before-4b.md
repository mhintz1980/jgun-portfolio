- [seen] Source coordinates in sections 4–6 are **zero-based UTF-16 character offsets, end exclusive**, measured from the local UTF-8 decoded production copies (Babel AST `start/end` or string indices), **not the approximate byte offsets in hotspots.md**. `J` means `C:/Users/Markimus/.buzz/REPOS/jgun-torque-wrench/research/oryzo/data/js/hoisted.CRsATKbF.js`; `C` means `C:/Users/Markimus/.buzz/REPOS/jgun-torque-wrench/research/oryzo/data/js/index.TL6TuoJb.css`; `W` means `C:/Users/Markimus/.buzz/REPOS/jgun-torque-wrench/research/oryzo/data/js/SplatsWorker-DSMxtdkh.js`. - [seen] Not a GSAP timeline/ScrollTrigger setup: `ScrollPane` smooths native scrolling, writes `window.scrollTo({behavior:"instant"})`, and preserves native body height. Wheel interpolation is `1-exp(-12*dt)` until distance <0.1 px; no fixed tween duration. Drag velocity uses the last 0.1 s of samples, friction interpolates 2.1→1.9 with speed/viewport/5, direction lock is true, multiplier 1. Native scrollbar events reset both current and target pixel positions and velocity. J chars 1048498–1055612.
- [seen] `ScrollManager` actually initializes `contentDom=#site-content`. Scrolling requires an active scroller, idle page manager, closed mobile menu and closed video overlay. Arrows move ±100 px; PageUp/PageDown move ±one viewport. A Home handler is **not present in this class**. J chars 1048498–1050260, 1055789–1057652.
- [seen] Snap-list storage and `getEaseInOutOffset` exist, but no authored call to `addToSnapList` or `getEaseInOutOffset` was found outside their definitions in J. The update loop does not consume `snapList`; do not describe active snap scrolling from these helpers alone. J chars 1050905–1051300, 1052305–1055612.
- [seen] Units below: `H=viewportHeight`; section-local `r=showScreenOffset=(H-section.screenY)/H`, so r=0 is section top at viewport bottom, r=1 is top at viewport top. `h=hideScreenOffset=-(screenY+height)/H`, so h=0 is section bottom at viewport top. J chars 402388–403825. `math.fit` clamps a normalized range and is linear when no callable ease is passed (J chars 386–466). No numeric ScrollTrigger `scrub` values apply; ranges are continuously sampled from the smoothed scroll position.
