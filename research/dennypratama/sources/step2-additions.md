## 6. 3D, shaders and runtime safeguards (Codex)

- **[seen] Renderer and shader inventory:** home uses a raw WebGL2 fullscreen triangle, not a mesh scene framework. The vertex shader simply forwards a 2D triangle position to clip space; it has **no vertex deformation, uniforms, lighting or textures**. The fragment program is `TUNNEL_FS`; source inputs are resolution, elapsed time, camera position/basis, ray tangent, tunnel visibility, end intensity, key offset, accent RGB and step budget. It reconstructs camera rays per fragment and colors an analytic distance-field scene; no model/texture fetch is needed for this program. Evidence: H@33400–34690; T@142–390. No additional GL shader is used by the four other preview engines inspected: Halden uses DOM/SVG, Noctua/Morrow/Fieldline use Canvas2D (HD@6800; NC@510; MW@1100; FL@500). Digital Revo uses DOM/SVG/video (RV@0–1700, RV@4750–5320).
- **[seen] Fragment geometry, described rather than copied:** an 11-vertex polygon and its point-reflected copy make the two halves of the logo's 2D SDF. The field has **13 indexed planes** spaced 6.4 units along negative Z, rotated by index*.2617994rad (15°), extrusion half-depth .17, bevel radius .014. Importantly, `KS=7` suppresses solids at indices1–6: those intermediate marks are **blueprint drawings**, not thirteen identical solid extrusions. The nearest-plane lookup, cross-plane distances and bounding cylinder radius1.03 / Z clipping avoid testing every mark with every ray. It marches up to `uSteps` with a hard shader ceiling220, step distance*.9, hit threshold .0004*max(distance,1), far distance80. Evidence: T@390–2400, T@2870–4520.
- **[seen] Blueprint shader pass:** after the solid intersection, rays intersect front/back planes of indices1–6; each receives a rotated logo outline, faint logo fill and an 8-cells-per-unit grid clipped to the logo. Line width scales with ray distance and `2*uTan/uRes.y` (1.25 factor), back planes weight .45, distance attenuation exp(−.03*distance), all multiplied by `uTun`. This is analytic overlay geometry in the fragment program, not a texture, displacement map or postprocessing compositor. Evidence: T@5610–6530.
- **[seen] Lighting/materials:** a warm gray albedo (.82,.80,.755), camera-relative point key at `camera+uKey`, inverse-quadratic-style attenuation `1/(1+.02*d²)`, Lambert diffuse gain2.4, hemisphere ambient and four-tap SDF ambient occlusion. Normals use four signed-distance probes, epsilon .0009. Specular uses exponent36 and strength.25. Slash-wall glow selects a narrow band using normal(−.4003,.9164), tangent(.9164,.4003), side-face normal weighting, accent*1.6 and blend weight.85; other side faces receive accent*.06. Driver accent is (.62,.60,.57), not an assumed saturated orange. No named PBR roughness/metalness or physically simulated shadow map exists in this shader. Evidence: T@2210–2870, T@4520–5630; H@37100–37320.
- **[seen] End light / post effects:** warm light at Z=−80 uses angular powers900,60,6000 with gains3,.16,8, attenuation1/(1+.0006*d²), tunnel visibility and `1+6*uEnd` brightness. Fog uses exp(−.028*distance), bleeding end light with exp(−.045*distance). The image blends to warm white by uEnd², gets exposure1.05, an ACES approximation (2.51,.03,2.43,.59,.14), gamma1/2.2, quadratic vignette `1−.5*dot(centeredUV,centeredUV)`, a minimum dark color and animated hash grain amplitude.03. All are in the **same fragment shader**, not separate bloom/DOF/SSAO passes. CSS adds a separate grain image and a radial exit flash. Evidence: T@6530–7550; C@45747; grain overlay remains documented in GLM section2.
- **[seen] Camera path:** section5 rows13–18 give the actual piecewise formula. Before p=.26 the camera aims at the origin while its spherical radius collapses; afterwards it looks down −Z, travels **linearly** to−76.8 over p=.26–.9, then smoothsteps four more units. `uCam` is constructed from forward/right/up, with no quaternion/Bezier path or FOV tween object. `uTan` .2679→.36 implies a vertical full angle of approximately30°→39.6° **[inferred from 2*atan(tan)]**. Evidence: H@36100–36950.
- **[seen] Pointer and device motion:** global pointer coordinates map to [−1,1] and damp at .05/ticker frame. They affect camera sway and key-light offset. Touch deviceorientation maps gamma/30 and (45−beta)/30, clamps±1; iOS permission is requested on a non-link/button hero click. `tunnel=force` overrides low-end/software/slow-frame gates, **not** reduced-motion or WebGL2 availability. Evidence: H@32500–33080, H@33400–34030, H@35280–35940, H@37100–37490.
- **[seen] Frame safeguards, corrected:** DPR is capped1.5, multiplied by qualityF=.62, then can drop to .52 and .42. Driver always supplies **uSteps=150**; the inspected source does **not** adapt the ray-step uniform. Idle detection uses exact unchanged scroll and pointer errors<.002, with a toggling guard that skips alternating settled draws, rather than stopping the scheduler entirely; time-based sway/grain can continue on drawn frames. Hidden document, offscreen hero, context failure or not-yet-ready GL skips drawing. Slow-frame logic averages elapsed time over J skipped/drawn intervals, exponential bad-frame ratio `.95*old+.05*(ms>34)`, threshold>.6 to reduce resolution; at lowest quality it falls back. Two consecutive measured intervals>250ms trigger fallback unless forced. Evidence: H@34690–35140, H@35800–36130, H@37150–37490.
- **[seen] Fallback / lifecycle:** reduced-motion or low-end non-iOS touch (≤4 cores or ≤3GB) skips GL but retains progress updates. Software renderer strings are rejected unless forced. Context loss switches to the still/story and restores corresponding story progress after two rAFs. ResizeObserver updates backing dimensions; abort handling prevents leaked shader setup; listeners/program/buffer are removed on cleanup. Evidence: H@32500–35140, H@35140–35940, H@37500–37740; C@46600–46900.
