- [seen] Smoothing uses elapsed-time exponential integration, with normalized intensity `i=intensity/100`; response is `0.22−0.12(i/0.5)` for `i≤0.5`, otherwise `0.1−0.07((i−0.5)/0.5)`. Each step mixes toward the target by `1−exp(−response×60×dt)`, snaps within **0.35 px**, and self-stops its rAF. Its dt is deliberately **uncapped** to catch up after stalls. No fixed duration or cubic-bezier is authored. [SC:111–163](<C:/Users/Markimus/.buzz/REPOS/jgun-torque-wrench/research/fragment/data/chunks/Yppqt3Cs3Y8TZqvASnXl__SmoothScroll_Prod.source.js:111>). [inferred] Substituting the mounted intensity 12 gives response **0.1912**, an approximate time constant **87.2 ms**; this is a derived response measure, not an animation duration.
- [seen] Wheel line deltas multiply by 16; page deltas multiply by viewport height; pixel deltas use 1. Target scroll is clamped to the document range. Nested scrollable panels retain ownership across a 150 ms gesture interval, including shadow-DOM paths.
