## The gap Mark named, and it is a real one

> *"I don't see any of the particle elements we discussed — mainly the wind, the air flowing
> through the enclosure, the two paths of air merging, flying off through the enclosure and
> out the end."*

Nothing was lost. The airflow is **fully specified in the approved plan and simply not built
yet** — it is build-order step 4, *"Add the engineering story"*, and the camera was step 3.

The spec is the plan's **"Flow, heat, and sound"** section
([JG-033 plan](../../plans/JG-033-rl300-quiet-machine.md) §145–153):

- **12–24 hero ribbons/streamlines** on desktop, **6–10** on mobile. Tapered tails,
  deliberately varied spacing, cores legible without bloom, tracer highlights as a secondary
  layer. The plan explicitly says this **replaces the "more dots" approach**.
- Route, verbatim: *beneath the skid → louvered liner `V2RL300-SAF-1047-5` → custom intake
  duct → outlet beneath the engine → upward past the engine → **shared exit with the main
  supply***. Show each supply independently before combining them.
- Heating **begins at the source region**, not a uniform rainbow across the enclosure. Heat
  distortion confined to the exhaust, dropped first on lower tiers.
- Sound gets **its own beat** — sparse fronts interacting with the sectioned acoustic system,
  not concentric bubbles. Keep the mount cue separate from airborne attenuation.
- Reversing scroll must retract the same geometry and return the same shot.
- No unsupported quantitative claims (no invented flow rates, temperatures, dB figures).
- The Blender source is **Z-up**, the web scene **Y-up**. Validate the conversion.

**Why the study looks completely airless:** the 12,000-particle `AirflowField` is alive in the
*portfolio* Station 2 (`/`, CH.03–04) — that is the air Mark remembers. The RL300 section
study at `?study=rl300` is a separate scene that mounts `Model`, `SectionCaps`, `LowerIntake`
and `Driver`, and nothing else. `grep -ri "airflow\|particle\|streamline" src/scene/rl300/`
returns nothing.

`src/scene/stages/AirflowField.tsx` and `airflowRoute.ts` are named in the plan as the
migration source, but read that as *provenance, not a copy*: a 12,000-dot field is the thing
the plan is replacing. `AcousticBaffleField.tsx` is to be inspected in detail during
implementation.

Shots 03, 05 and 06 are already framed and captioned for this and currently illustrate
nothing — shot 05 is literally titled "Two feeds, one exit". A TODO entry now carries this
spec inline so it cannot stay buried in a plan paragraph.
