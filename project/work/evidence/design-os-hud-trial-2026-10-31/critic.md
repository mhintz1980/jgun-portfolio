## Recommendation

Take A forward as the model-first visual starting point, with a more explicit chapter control and a genuinely discoverable inspection action. Its mobile inspection still keeps the important colored shaft visible and caption below. C offers the clearest mobile chapter affordance but needs a different caption position at the shaft shot. ## A — Museum caption

- **First seen:** In `narrow/p-0.7350-concept-A.png`, the bright colored shaft reads first, then the large Inspection caption; in `desktop/p-0.9400-concept-A.png`, the exploded assembly dominates before the lower-left caption. The large type is calmer and easier to parse than the dense narrative in `narrow/p-0.7350-control.png`.
- **Discoverability:** The underlined Explore details reads as an action, but the tiny 01–05 rail is ambiguous as navigation and gives no chapter names. Free Look / Inspect and Pages are very small, particularly at the bottom of the narrow image. In contrast, the control has a clearly bounded Free Look / Inspect button. The concept stills do not prove these affordances work.
- **Occlusion:** The final `desktop/p-0.7350-concept-A.png` shows the shaft clearly, with the caption below and to its left. `desktop/p-0.9400-concept-A.png` places the caption over the lower-left edge of the dark assembly, while the rail sits over the background. `desktop/p-0.4180-concept-A.png` places the caption over the drawing sheet, reducing its contrast and covering some drawing detail. `narrow/p-0.4180-concept-A.png` puts the gold rail over the right side of the sheet and near the wrench outline. Move or soften the rail where the scene is bright; avoid increasing caption footprint.
- **Mobile reflow:** The lower caption in `narrow/p-0.7350-concept-A.png` avoids the shaft and the lower caption in `narrow/p-0.9400-concept-A.png` sits below the gears. The brand at top overlaps the silver shaft in the former. Give the brand a small, restrained contrast treatment or relocate it where necessary; test tall subjects before fixing the placement.
- **Tradeoff:** Strong scene hierarchy and useful story title, but chapter meaning and the inspection action are easy to miss. Add a modest labeled Chapters control and increase the salience and touch area of Free Look / Inspect, preserving the open central field.

## B — Technical margin

## C — Minimal transport

- **First seen:** `desktop/p-0.9400-concept-C.png` shows the exploded model with a compact upper-left title and a low centered transport; `narrow/p-0.4180-concept-C.png` presents a clear story title above the drawing and a recognizable Chapters bar below.
- **Discoverability:** The framed Chapters control is the clearest mobile chapter affordance of the three, and the desktop transport makes the existence of multiple chapters more obvious than A's bare numeric rail. Other chapters are mostly numbers, however, and small Free Look / Inspect and Pages labels remain weak next to the control's visible button. The still does not establish whether the Chapters control opens or its contents are usable.
- **Occlusion:** `narrow/p-0.7350-concept-C.png` puts the Inspection title, explanatory line and Explore details directly over the bright shaft; portions of the copy lose contrast and the UI interrupts the model. `desktop/p-0.9400-concept-C.png` keeps the caption relatively clear, although the bottom transport occupies part of the image field. The drawing stays legible in `narrow/p-0.4180-concept-C.png`.
- **Mobile reflow:** The desktop horizontal transport becomes a full-width Chapters bar above the footer, an understandable adaptation. The fixed upper-left caption works for the drawing and exploded assembly but fails for the tall central shaft. Try A's lower caption placement at this state, with a compact contrasting surface only where needed.
- **Tradeoff:** Best visible mobile navigation and a clean central field in two sampled states, but the shaft collision is prominent. Preserve the Chapters affordance while making caption positioning responsive to the actual scene and keeping the inspect action visible.
