# v2 HUD concepts — D (A+ museum caption) and E (C+ minimal transport) — 2026-11-02

Continuation of the 2026-10-31 trial after the independent critic's review. The v1 packet
(control + A/B/C) is unchanged; this session added two refined concepts that implement the
critic's recommendation and fixes, plus a both-ways treatment of the technical inspection
content. HUD direction remains **awaiting Mark** — this packet exists to make that choice visual.

## Concepts

**D / A+ museum caption** (primary, per the critic's "take A forward"):
- Named chapter rail replaces A's bare 01–05 numbers: dimmed names always visible, active
  chapter white with gold rule, locked number/name columns, over a strengthened right-edge
  scrim (critic: rail ambiguous; numbers indistinguishable on the bright shaft/sheet).
- Per-state caption anchoring: Breakthrough/Drafting captions move to the upper-left dark
  zone under the mast (critic: 0.418 caption over the sheet lost contrast; frame analysis:
  top band is the quiet zone); Inspection/Assembly stay low-left, Assembly over a subtle
  wash; narrow keeps the low caption with a wash when the sheet is bright.
- Bounded Free Look / Inspect button (42 px desktop / 44 px narrow touch target) with
  border + blur backing; Pages stays a quiet text secondary (critic: affordances tiny).
- Inspection technical cue under the lede — `INPUT SHAFT P001835 · TRIED AISI 4140 / 4340 /
  C300` / `MIN FOS < 1.0 AT THE RELIEF GROOVE — FAILED` — real copy from the shaft story
  (`ShaftStoryLayer.tsx`), plus a gold elbow leader from the caption to the shaft's stress
  band (critic: technical evidence must stay reachable; caption "could describe anything").
- Narrow: labeled Chapters bar above the footer (C's affordance, the critic's mobile pick)
  instead of the right rail.

**E / C+ minimal transport** (secondary, quietest chrome):
- C's base; the caption drops low-left **only** for Inspection (critic's fix for the
  caption-over-shaft collision), hover/focus-revealed chapter names in the transport, the
  same bounded Inspect button, the same tech cue/leader system.

**Tech content, both routes** (Mark picks from images, per the owner policy that text alone
should not stand in for a visual choice): `?tech=cue` (default) keeps chrome quiet with the
one-line cue + full story in the details drawer; `?tech=inline` keeps a compact evidence
strip visible (material attempts with FAILED tags + FOS 0–3 bar with sub-1.0 marker + revised
4340 note). The FOS bar colors approximate the shared `FOS_STOPS` gradient for concept
purposes; no numbers are fabricated — all copy traces to `ShaftStoryLayer.tsx` / `Story.tsx`.
