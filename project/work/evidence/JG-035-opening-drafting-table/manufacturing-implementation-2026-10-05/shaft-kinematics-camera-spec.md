Kinematics (sampleShaftKinematics(t, out)):
- Machining-time map m(t): continuous, monotonic, piecewise-smooth (C1) function of the master playhead. Shaping 2..15 s: normal speed 2..6, ease down into a slow interval covering the cutter exit (>= 1.2 s of stable-camera slow action within 6..11 including ~0.4 s of unmistakable clearance during the relieved return), ease back up, then a visibly time-compressed recap 11..15 that completes all remaining tooth spaces before 15 s. Hobbing 25..32: feed hob centre from -4.195 to 9.5249 mm, retract 2.5 mm radially and withdraw so the hob is clear and out of the runout view by 32.0 s.
- Shaper: cutter rotation phi_c(m), work rotation phi_w = -2 * phi_c (signed ratio work/cutter -2), stroke y(m) between the certified stroke centre limits (cutting toward +Y, relieved return backed off 2.0 mm), radial infeed reaching full depth during the first quarter orbit then holding. Per tooth-space depth depth_i(t) in [0,1] derived from engagement: a space gains depth only when it passes the cutter mesh point while the cutter is cutting; expose spaceDepth[10], engagedSpace, edgeY consistent with progression.ts semantics (Float32Array owned by the caller). All 10 spaces reach depth 1 by 15.0 s.
- Hob: rotation phi_h(m), work rotation phi_w = phi_h / 10 (single start, 10 teeth; sign documented), hobYc(m), hobA(m).
- Chip: one restrained chip at the shaper rake face only while stock is removed (closed-form position/curl/opacity from stroke phase; seeded deterministically); none during return.
- Display-rate guard: stroke and gash frequencies on screen must stay <= one quarter of 30 fps, or expose a 'softened' flag for the renderer to blur/fade repeated features; test it over the timeline.
- Tests: m(t) continuous and monotonic (dense 1/240 sweep), signed pitch-point relative velocity of shaper generating rolling is ~0 (record tolerance), all spaces complete by 15 s and none before being engaged, no reversal of displayed rotation at the lite floor, hob clear by 32.0 s, determinism under shuffled seeks, allocation-free.
