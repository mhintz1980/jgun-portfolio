Recorded 2026-10-04. This documents a proposed revision to the existing Ring Switch camera/light study. The owner's latest instruction is to document the request and write the handoff only. ## Requested sequence

1. Zoom toward the Ring Switch. Everything else fades into darkness, leaving only the switch visible with its initial smooth black finish.
2. As the background disappears, transition the ring's black finish to raw aluminium and start the ring spinning rapidly.
3. A scissor-style knurling tool approaches and contacts the ring's outside diameter (OD).
4. The tool traverses down the OD face for approximately one or two seconds.
5. The tool retracts and exits the frame, leaving a fresh knurl pattern around the OD.
6. Transition the OD back to dark black as the rest of the assembled tool fades back into view.

The existing concept packet identifies the Ring Switch as P003068. The owner used both “iSwitch” and “Ring Switch” in the request; this note treats them as the same intended subject rather than introducing another part.

## Optional refinements discussed by the parent

These are suggestions, not additional owner requirements or reviewed consensus:

- Reveal the knurl progressively behind the rollers as the tool travels, making the contact and resulting finish easy to follow.
- Preserve a recognizable paired scissor-tool mechanism, plausible rolling contact and consistent ring geometry throughout the shot.
- Resolve the ring's support and spin axis before depicting the machining action. Establish whether the intended traverse is axial across the OD width using the supplied references and actual part geometry.
- Treat the black → aluminium → black sequence as cinematic compression of manufacture and finishing. The returning black finish should retain the knurl relief.

Only the traverse has a requested duration (approximately one or two seconds). Total duration, camera path, support/fixture, tool entry direction, spin speed and sound remain undecided. Do not carry over the old video's ten-second duration as an owner requirement.
