1. **Lightning "Picture-in-Picture" (PIP) Bug:**
   - *Cause:* In [`ThunderLight.tsx`](file:///c:/Users/Markimus/.buzz/REPOS/jgun-torque-wrench/src/scene/studiomark-logo/ThunderLight.tsx), a physical 3D plane mesh (`<mesh scale={[22, 10, 1]}><planeGeometry /></mesh>`) was placed in world space behind the text to simulate a distant sky glow, appearing as a rectangular PIP card when struck.
   - *Fix:* Completely removed the planar mesh. Implemented full-scene volumetric atmospheric lightning via an overhead cold directional flash (`#b5d7ff`), a storm hemisphere light burst (`#9ec5ff` to `#1e2738`), and an ambient surge. Zero rectangular artifacts remain.
2. **"Wave Ripple Distortion" Slider Restored:**
   - *Cause:* Three.js's standard `MeshPhysicalMaterial` was not propagating runtime ripple offsets to mirror reflections.
   - *Fix:* Connected Drei's `<MeshReflectorMaterial>` with dynamic `distortion={rainEnabled ? rippleStrength * 0.12 : 0.0}`, `distortionMap={rippleNormalMap}`, `normalMap={rippleNormalMap}`, and `normalScale={new THREE.Vector2(rippleStrength * 1.8, rippleStrength * 1.8)}`. Dragging the slider now immediately modulates wave amplitude from glassy calm (0.0) to turbulent storm ripples (0.80) in real-time.
3. **Water Puddle Quality & Real-Time Reflections Restored:**
   - Re-enabled planar mirror reflections of the glowing extruded 3D "STUDIOMARK" typography and rotating centerpiece in the puddles.
   - Fixed the giant white glare rectangle on the floor caused by an oversized rear softbox in [`StudioLighting.tsx`](file:///c:/Users/Markimus/.buzz/REPOS/jgun-torque-wrench/src/scene/studiomark-logo/StudioLighting.tsx) by repositioning it into a high-angle rim backlight.
