## Input Shaft storyline — owner text, verbatim

> For the animation that points out the change of machining process from gear shaper to rotary hobb we can do something similar to the Ring Switch animation we will be doing which involves fading everything to black so only the Input Shaft remains and highlighting the undercut and showing a gear cutter, disc style that is standard tooling in a gear shaping machine, orbiting the input shaft as it cuts the teeth and working its way to the full tool depth. We only need to show a portion of the orbit, the cutter quickly moving up and down the tooth profile and rotating on it's own axis as it orbits the input shaft. After a quarter of an orbit we do a zoom in as the motion slows down (like a movie scene where the audio slows and distorts with the video, like "Bullet TIme" in "The Matrix".). When zoomed in close enough the viewer can see the cutter reaching the end of the face width of the gear it's cutting and protruding out the bottom of the surface before moving back up the tooth to start the next cut. The user can clearly see why the Input shaft has the narrow undercut below the gear...So the gear cutter could cut the tooth and excavate a metal chip into the clearance the undercut provides. We zoom back a bit and show the cutter traversing up and down the face width rapidly on its slow orbit-like toolpath. then it fades away leaving the Input Shaft alone in the scene with the undercut highlighted in a the colors of a failed FEA; red, orange gradient effect showing where the stress on the sun gear would snap the part at the undercut. In large text to the side of the Input Shaft we display "AISI 4140 (40-45 HRC)", then below it "FAILED" with a thump and in the color and style like it was stamped onto the screen. The text fades away and then in its place comes AISI 4340 (48-50 HRC)" and then below it another "FAILED" gets stamped. Repeat again with C300 (56-58 HRC), and "FAILED" appears again. The text fades and we now, with the Input Shaft alone on screen again, we fade in the hobbed version of the Input Shaft, but without the gears cut in it. The geared end looks like a sturdy smooth shaft. Then, like the gear cutter before we fade in a rotary hobb tool and show it cutting the gear teeth as the input shaft rotates on its own axis completely cutting the gear on the cylindrical shaft without needing the undercut. The FEA scan highlights the entire directly below the geared section with shades of green and blue and next to it appears the text AISI 4340 (H.T. 48-50 HRC). Fade in the bearing K000210, retaining ring K000211 and 1st & 2nd St housing P000725 and animate the bearing and retaining ring to there new position slightly down the Input Shaft from the original location. The Input Shaft, planets and cages fade in and start spinning again and then fade in the rest of the model as well.
