**Core Mechanical Anatomy & Subassemblies**

* **Multi-Stage Planetary Gearbox (Front Assembly):**
* **Cylindrical Gearcase:** Heavy-duty, high-gloss cylindrical casing containing a high-ratio planetary gear train to multiply motor torque.
* **Output Anvil:** Hardened industrial square drive spindle (3/4" or 1" format) protruding from the forward nose cone for heavy-duty impact/torque socket retention.
* **Reaction Spline Collar:** Knurled/splined coaxial collar positioned immediately behind the square drive to lock reaction arms or backup washer reaction fixtures in place against counter-rotational torque.


* **Drive & Motor Housing (Mid-Section):**
* **Motor Casing:** Cylindrical upper body housing the internal drive motor, seamlessly merging into the lower handle casting.
* **Branding & Graphics:** High-contrast crisp white silkscreen/laser marking on the drive barrel displaying `TORQUE GUN` (with a stylized sheared `O` icon) and the sub-brand `— A HYTORC COMPANY —`.
* **Directional Shuttle Switch:** Small toggle/slider switch located on the left flank just above the trigger for forward/reverse directional switching.


* **Digital Telemetry & Control Interface (Rear Endcap):**
* **Rear Bezel:** Angled, beveled backplate secured to the motor cylinder via perimeter hex-socket cap screws.
* **Display Module:** Recessed instrument panel housing dual backlit LCD/OLED multi-segment digital readouts showing real-time torque, angle, or target presets.
* **Tactile Keypad:** Membrane control cluster beneath the screen featuring tactile buttons: Power/Standby (`⑂`), Preset cycle, and Clockwise / Counter-Clockwise (`↻` / `↺`) rotation indicator icons.


* **Ergonomic Handle & Power Supply (Lower Assembly):**
* **Pistol Grip:** Contoured ergonomic handle designed to resist rotational kickback and provide stable single-handed manipulation.
* **Throttle Lever:** Index-finger mechanical trigger switch for throttle and speed regulation.
* **Supply Inlet:** Nickel-plated/stainless steel quick-disconnect male pneumatic nipple coupling extending from the base of the grip for high-pressure air line connection.
